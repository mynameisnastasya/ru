06 / FAQ

Remove the doubt
before the DM.

What if I do not know which colour direction fits me?

Use the four directions as a starting point, then send daylight photos of your roots. The goal of the builder is to make the conversation clearer — not to force you into a preset.

Can I ask for something outside these four looks?

Yes. The directions are reference points. Your brief can combine a different transition, texture, length or contrast before you confirm the final order with GuruDreads.

Is this the final checkout?

No. This concept helps you define and communicate a custom direction. Final price, availability, specifications and timing should be confirmed through GuruDreads’ official channels.
