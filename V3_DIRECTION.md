# WINK V3 direction

## Reset decision
V2 was rejected because the art direction became more important than the product: parchment/editorial surfaces, excessive explanatory whitespace and a magazine-like composition weakened the emotional gifting proposition.

## V3 product promise
WINK is a premium gifting studio in Kemerovo that removes difficult choice. The user gives context; WINK returns up to two appropriate solutions, then personalization and delivery are confirmed.

## Art direction
Product-first luxury object commerce.
- White / bone canvas, deep aubergine ink.
- Photography is the dominant source of color.
- Zero shadows, glassmorphism and decorative gradients.
- Instrument Serif is reserved for emotional campaign moments; Inter carries interface and operational copy.
- Hairline rules, square corners, small uppercase labels.
- Images are large, architectural and asymmetrically sequenced.
- Motion changes scale/depth and state; it never exists as decoration.

## Reference logic
- Refero luxury object systems: product as object, quiet UI, bone/ink surfaces, serif + restrained grotesk.
- Siteinspire Panconesi / Aesop patterns: product and photography dominate, UI recedes.
- WINK-specific conversion logic remains: WINK MATCH is the primary path; curated edit is secondary; full catalog remains available.

## V3 homepage sequence
1. Product + promise in the first viewport.
2. One-line positioning statement.
3. WINK MATCH as a black concierge scene.
4. Three curated ready-made scenarios.
5. Personalization as a single visual split.
6. Operational proof in a dense ledger.
7. FAQ.
8. Image-led closing CTA.

## Guardrails
- No manifesto sections that do not change user understanding.
- No repeated three-card marketing grids.
- No fake social proof.
- No WebGL unless real assets make it useful.
- Do not touch checkout/order business logic for visual reasons.
