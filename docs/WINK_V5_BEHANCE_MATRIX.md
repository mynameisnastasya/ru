# WINK V5 — Behance reference matrix

V5 is based on recurring patterns across 30+ Behance web, e-commerce, luxury, beauty, floral and gifting projects rather than one visual reference.

## Highest-signal references

- AURELIA — Fine Jewelry E-commerce UI/UX: calm premium world, warm neutral palette, clean composition, product confidence.
- ÉRRA ATELIER — Luxury Fashion E-commerce: editorial rhythm combined with conversion-focused commerce.
- VOXA — Luxury Watch Landing Page: high contrast and large product presentation without losing navigation clarity.
- UDS Website — Studio Twoo: imagery leads, interface stays minimal and structural.
- POISE — E-commerce: restrained fashion-commerce composition and visual identity.
- Here & Away — Web Design: immersive photography-led storytelling with intuitive navigation.
- Cosmetics E-commerce + AI Visuals — Marina Pereverzeva: cohesive campaign visual system plus clear store pages.
- E-commerce Website for Skincare Brand — Shakuro: brand intro → benefits → product → trust → commerce.
- MUR MUR Flowers — E-commerce Website: light, calm, emotionally resonant floral commerce.
- Landing page design / flower shop — Darya Karpovich: visually clean marketing-first structure without animation overload.
- Flowwow Rebranding: emotional brand world around gifting while preserving product-system clarity.

## Recurring patterns adopted for WINK

1. One dominant visual per major scene instead of decorative effects competing for attention.
2. Neutral canvas with one restrained brand accent; product photography supplies most of the color.
3. Large typography with moderate weights and tight tracking, not shouty all-caps poster text.
4. Asymmetric editorial grids built on strict alignment and spacing.
5. Minimal card chrome: borders and hierarchy replace shadows, glass and bento decoration.
6. Product imagery occupies more area than descriptive copy.
7. Alternation between immersive full-width moments and dense commercial information.
8. Clear price/action hierarchy remains visible inside premium art direction.
9. Motion is slow reveal, image scale, crossfade and state transition; no continuous spectacle.
10. Mobile uses its own composition: short first-screen promise, image crops, horizontal product rail, large touch targets.

## Explicitly rejected

- hot-pink poster/brutalism as the whole identity;
- beige editorial magazine treatment;
- giant all-caps words used as decoration;
- black sections solely to signal “premium”;
- repeated three-card grids;
- gradients/glass/noise without product meaning;
- heavy WebGL or scroll choreography with no conversion purpose.
